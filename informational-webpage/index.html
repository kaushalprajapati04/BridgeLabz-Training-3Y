History of Paris

Paris, founded in the 3rd century BC, is one of the world’s most historic and culturally rich cities. From medieval times to the modern era, Paris has been a global center of art, fashion, and philosophy.

Parisian Culture

Paris is known for its cafés, museums, literature, and world-famous architectural style. The city’s unique charm attracts millions of visitors from around the globe.

Top Attractions

Eiffel Tower, Louvre Museum, Notre-Dame Cathedral, and the Seine River are just a few of the breathtaking attractions Paris offers.
